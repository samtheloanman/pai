## 1. Visual Atmosphere & Philosophy

- **Density:** 4/10 ("Art Gallery Airy" with surgical Scandinavian restraint).
- **Variance:** 7/10 (Confident offset asymmetry; generous spatial zones; zero cookie-cutter cards).
- **Motion:** 6/10 (Hardware-accelerated spring physics: `stiffness: 100, damping: 20`; weighty tactile responses).
- **Atmosphere:** An architectural, bespoke domestic technology brand. It evokes the silent physical elegance of Teenage Engineering, Braun, and Dieter Rams, fused with modern monochromatic typography and calibrated botanical teal accents. It feels permanently grounded in the home—calm, private, and utterly free of AI hype or neon clichés.

## 3. Typographic Architecture

- **Primary Display & Headlines:** `Geist` (or `Instrument Sans`) — Track-tight (`-0.03em`), optical weight hierarchy. Controlled scale, never screaming.
- **Body Text:** `Geist` — Relaxed line height (`1.6`), max line-width of 65 characters (`max-w-[65ch]`), warm slate ink.
- **Technical & Hardware Specs:** `Geist Mono` or `JetBrains Mono` — Tabular figures for benchmark metrics, RAM allocation, and token latency.
- **Banned Typography:** `Inter` is banned for brand and hardware identity. Generic serifs (`Times New Roman`, `Georgia`, `Palatino`) are banned.

## 5. Computer Hardware 3D Rendering Blueprint

To replace placeholder illustrations with photorealistic industrial renders matching Apple Pro hardware standards:

### 5.1 Physical Chassis Specifications
- **Form Factor:** Compact monolithic cube/slab (135mm × 135mm × 42mm). Machined anodized aerospace-grade aluminum (Space Gray or Bead-Blasted Matte Silver).
- **Top Surface:** CNC-milled matte metal with a laser-etched PAI sovereign hearth logo.
- **Front Panel:** Clean unbroken metal with:
  - Single flush USB-C 40Gbps port.
  - Micro-perforated diffused LED halo (soft `#3DD6C6` bioluminescent glow when listening/thinking; dark when dormant). Zero bright blinking gamer LEDs.
- **Rear I/O Panel:**
  - OCuLink PCIe 4.0 x4 high-speed expansion port (for GPU Dock).
  - Dual 2.5GbE Ethernet jacks (isolated WAN/LAN).
  - 2× USB-A 3.2, 1× HDMI 2.1, DC power barrel.
- **Bottom:** CNC ventilation slots with precision acoustic baffling for silent (<22 dB) operation.
